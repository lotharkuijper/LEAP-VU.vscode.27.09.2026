# Projecten, groepen en persona's

Besluiten en werking van LEAP op dit gebied, zoals ze in de oude CLAUDE.md stonden. Geen instructies die altijd gelden, maar naslag: lees dit als je aan dit onderdeel werkt, en werk het bij als een besluit verandert.

## Projects, groups and project bots (personas)

Group content (group chat, persona conversations, sessions, feedback, relationship, group files) is for group members and LEAP admins only: `isLeapAdmin(profile)` plus membership (`isGroupMember` in `server/projects/access.js`), never `isStaffForCourse`. Full privacy model: `docs/decisions/privacy-en-data.md`.

### Setting up a project in steps; draft projects (2026-11-11)

- A teacher sets up a project in six steps, like Course material: the assignment (`ProjectTaskStep`), material, feedback and assessment (`ProjectFeedbackSetup`; evaluators are created, edited and deleted only here), characters (talking personas only, optional), groups (`ProjectGroupsStep`), ready for students (`ProjectReadyStep`). For an open project there is also "During the project" (submissions with marking comments, groups). The step bar and the checklist come from `setupIssues` in `src/pages/admin/ProjectSetupSteps.tsx`; items marked `optional` (no material, no characters, a persona that knows nothing, no feedback choice yet, Canvas not yet linked) never count as a problem, so a teacher can skip the groups step when the course is not linked to Canvas.
- Assessment before characters (decided 2026-11-11): what counts (rubric, feedback, marking) frames the project, so it comes before the characters that lead there (constructive alignment). A rubric uploaded under Material points to Feedback and assessment. Characters are optional: a project without talking personas is valid (for example marking only); the step bar then says "Geen (optioneel)". There is no fallback persona any more: the room and `POST /api/projects/persona-chat` used to offer a hidden default "Consultant" when a project had no personas; that is gone, and the student room then says the project has no conversation partner.
- The feedback choice is stored (decided 2026-11-11): `projects.feedback_mode` = `marking` | `both` | `feedback` | NULL (migration `20261111300000_project_feedback_mode` + rollback; set via `PATCH /api/projects/:id`, validated against the same three values). NULL (older projects) means "derive from the evaluators that exist" (`effectiveFeedbackMode`). The step status follows the choice: marking chosen without a marking assistant, or with submissions off, and feedback chosen without a feedback provider, are real issues; feedback providers left over after choosing marking only is an issue too (students could still use them); a marking assistant left over after choosing feedback only is optional. Choosing a mode with marking turns submissions on at the same time.
- The marking assistant is not a conversation partner (decided 2026-11-11): it only writes marking comments for the teacher after a submission, so it is left out of the persona list and the consultations overview for everyone, teachers included, and `POST /api/projects/persona-chat` refuses it (404). Its rubric, when visible to students, is still listed in the room under Beoordelingscriteria, without its name or avatar (each rubric once). While a project is a draft, the teacher sees the test submissions with their marking comments under step 3 ("Uitproberen: jouw proefinleveringen"); once the project is open they are under Tijdens het project as before.
- A new project starts as `status = 'draft'` (migration `20261111100000_project_draft` + rollback; existing projects stay `active`). Only teachers of the course and LEAP admins can use a draft: the read rule on `projects` hides it from students; `userHasProjectAccess` refuses non-staff; `isGroupMember` only counts a member of a draft project if they are teacher of the course or admin (`mayUseDraftProject`), so students already placed in a group (by the teacher or Canvas) get in only once the project is opened; creating, joining or restarting a group and the Canvas launch are refused for students. The student overview lists drafts only for teachers/admins, with "Try it out in your own test group" whatever the group mode. Opening and back to draft go through `PATCH /api/projects/:id` (`status` limited to draft/active/archived). The database rules do the same: the `projects` read rule hides a draft from everyone but course staff (`pr_is_course_staff`), `pr_is_group_member` only counts a member of a draft project if they are course staff (so group chat, persona conversations, files and members of a draft are closed to students), and the member branches of `project_personas_select` and `project_documents_select` skip drafts. In a draft the teacher can hand in and ask for feedback in the own test group (`projectAcceptsWork`). Tests: `server/__tests__/projectDraftAccess.test.js`, `scripts/local-db/tests/project-draft.sql`. The rollback turns remaining drafts into `archived`, not `active`, so no unfinished project suddenly appears for students.
- A teacher never ends up in a group with students through group management (`server/projects/projectGroups.js`): automatic division keeps only students of the course (`studentsOfCourse`), and placing a student in a group that holds a course teacher (the teacher's test group) is refused (`groupHasTeacher`). `server/__tests__/projectGroupsTeacherGuard.test.js`.
- The marking assistant is its own bot (decided 2026-11-11): step 3 of Feedback and assessment never offers feedback providers to pick from, because picking one silently took it away from step 2. Without a marking assistant there is only "Add marking assistant"; turning a marking assistant into a feedback provider is an explicit action.

### Project bots (personas)
- Three roles in `persona_type`:
  - `conversational` = Begeleider (guide);
  - `evaluator` = Beoordelaar (formative feedback on submitted work with the rubric, `max_reviews` rounds per group, `deliverable_label`);
  - `roleplayer` = Rolspeler (character in a simulation).

  Field rules live in `server/projects/personaRoles.js` (`roleFieldsFrom`, `copyRoleFields`). Every create/copy/patch route for templates and project copies goes through them. The admin UI uses `PersonaRoleFields` for both editors.
- A relationship ("verstandhouding") exists ONLY for a role player with `reputation_enabled` (`reputationActive`). Guides and evaluators never get one, and the room shows no label for them.
- Levels are stored in `project_persona_relationships.score`: −2 cold … +2 warm, and −3 = contact broken.
  - After each closed conversation, a separate LLM judgement with the teacher's `conduct_rules` moves the level by at most one step (`server/projects/threadClose.js`, `threadCloseService.js`).
  - A negative step from cold breaks contact. The role player then no longer answers, and only a teacher correction (`relationship-adjust`, `{ level, note }`, `server/projects/relationshipAdjust.js`) restores it.
  - The conduct rules are never put in the persona's chat prompt; it only gets its level and the matching behaviour.
  - Students see the level and the latest reason.
- What a persona knows (2026-10-27, `server/projects/personaKnowledge.js`, `PersonaKnowledgeFields`, migration `20261027120000_persona_knowledge` + rollback). Per persona the teacher chooses, hidden from students:
  - `course_knowledge` `none|all|selected` (+ `course_knowledge_doc_ids`, course RAG documents only);
  - `project_knowledge_doc_ids` (project documents). New personas know NOTHING (only their instructions); `rag_enabled`/`rag_folder_ids` are no longer read.
  - Project documents a chat persona knows are chunked into `document_chunks` under their `document_ref_id` (Projectdata folder, outside the course RAG folders, so tutor/quiz/concepts never see them) and searched per question (`searchChunksServerSide(…, { documentIds })`, `server/rag/search.js`). Visible documents get a source number; hidden ones are background knowledge without a source. Not yet indexed → the start of the text goes along and indexing runs in the background.
  - Data files (`material_kind` `data`, or a data extension without another kind) are NEVER persona knowledge; the server filters them and removes a document from every persona when it is deleted or becomes data.
  - Evaluators read their documents in full (`loadEvaluatorMaterial`) for feedback rounds, "Feedback op jullie aanpak" (was "Beoordeling opvragen") and the marking comments (nakijkcommentaar); the rubric is a project document of kind `rubric` (old `project_persona_documents.is_hidden_rubric` rows were migrated and are no longer read). Every persona copy/create route uses `copyKnowledgeFields` / `knowledgeFieldsFrom` + `sanitizePersonaKnowledge` (`server/projects/personaRoutes.js`); `server/__tests__/personaKnowledge.test.js` guards this.
- Chatting with an evaluator (decided 2026-11-11, replaces "students do not chat with evaluators"): evaluators are in the students' persona list (not the pre-assessor; `system_prompt` and knowledge fields are stripped for students). In the chat the evaluator is only a guide for handing in work, in their own name and tone (`server/projects/evaluatorChat.js`): what to hand in, where and how, the group's real feedback rounds, whether the final product is in, allowed files, which rubric the group can open. It gives no content feedback or verdict there, does not reveal rubric content or its own assessment instructions (its `system_prompt` and knowledge are not loaded), and says it does not know what the facts do not contain (refer to the teacher). It uses no course material and no uploads (the upload button is hidden; the server already refuses student uploads to an evaluator) and does not count against the consultation limit. Only course staff can chat with the pre-assessor.
- Feedback from an evaluator is purely formative (decided 2026-11-10): no grade, no verdict, no badge. Only what already works, where the most is to be gained and a next step, so there is nothing to "get to green" and tweaking towards the final assessment pays off less. Prompt: `formative_feedback` in `chatbot_prompts` (section `project`, default `DEFAULT_FORMATIVE_FEEDBACK_PROMPT`); the old `document_review` prompt is no longer read. `validateReviewResponse` drops any grade or verdict; new rows leave `verdict`/`grade` empty. Migration `20261110100000_formative_feedback` (+ rollback) moved old grades, verdicts, raw model answers, stored journal texts and badges to backup tables without read rules; `reviewEntry` rebuilds journal texts without a grade. The backup tables cascade with the account (badges, also in the data export `server/account/myData.js`) and with the review (grades). The migration moves the old `document_review` prompt row to `chatbot_prompts_backup` and adds a partial unique index for achievements without a level (`student_achievements_unique_nolevel`). Deliberately kept for the rollback: the columns `project_document_reviews.verdict`/`grade` (always empty for new rows), `badge_award_mode` on `project_personas`/`course_personas` (no longer read or written) and the empty table `project_review_badges`.
- Groups submit work to an evaluator via `POST …/groups/:groupId/personas/:personaId/feedback` (stored in `project_group_products`, reviewed by `runEvaluatorReview` in `server/projects/evaluatorReview.js`). A round only counts when feedback was produced (`feedbackRoundsState`, `server/projects/documentReview.js`). "Feedback op jullie aanpak" (`POST /api/projects/groups/:groupId/evaluate`) counts as one round at every evaluator that takes part, and evaluators whose rounds are used up are skipped (`countFeedbackRounds`): the teacher sets one maximum per product. Evaluator feedback does not change any relationship. There is no other route that produces evaluator feedback. A round is reserved before the model call (the uploaded product row, or an empty `project_group_assessments` row that the room hides) and the count is checked again after reserving, so parallel requests cannot exceed the maximum; a failed call releases the round. Only group members and LEAP admins may call "Feedback op jullie aanpak" (it uses the group's conversations and rounds; guarded in `teacherPrivacy.test.js`). Evaluator material that students cannot see is marked confidential in the prompt (`buildEvaluatorMaterial`) and student work is delimited as material, not instructions.
- Rubric criteria are public (decided 2026-11-10): students may know what they are assessed on. The same rubric serves the formative feedback (students) and the marking comments (teacher); the difference is the prompt and who sees the result. Independence of the final judgement comes from: no grades during the project, limited rounds, a fresh reading of the whole product by the marking comments without the earlier feedback, and the teacher who assesses.
- Feedback on the final reflection (`kind = final` checkpoint) is about the reflection itself (what the group learned, how it worked together, its choices), not about the product or the project rubric points, and has no verdicts (`journalFromReflectionFeedback`).
- Badge for the best relationship (2026-11-10, `server/projects/reputationBadge.js`): when a conversation lifts a group to the highest level (+2, warm) with a role player for the first time, every group member gets a `student_achievements` row of kind `reputation` (topic_key = persona id, one per student per role player). A teacher correction never earns it. `server/__tests__/reputationBadge.test.js`.
- Marking comments (nakijkcommentaar; in code still "pre-assessment") on the final product (2026-10-16, renamed 2026-11-10, `server/projects/preassessment.js`, migration `20261016140000_project_preassessment` with rollback):
  - The final product is submitted once per group (2026-11-11, `uploadProjectSubmissionHandler`): the student first picks the file, then confirms; a second upload gets 409 `alreadySubmitted`. The check runs after locking the group row, so two members cannot both submit. Only in a draft does a new upload replace the old one (the teacher tries it out). To let a group submit again, the teacher deletes the submission. The marking assistant's editor has no feedback-rounds field. `server/__tests__/projectSubmissionOnce.test.js`.
  - A teacher designates one Evaluator per project as pre-assessor (`projects.preassessment_persona_id`, `PUT …/preassessment-persona`). After every submission (`project_submissions`) the server reads the WHOLE product (in parts of ~18k characters plus a synthesis call) and stores comments per criterion of that evaluator's rubric in `project_submission_assessments`: strengths, concerns, quotes and an indication (`strong|adequate|weak|unclear`). Never a grade; the teacher assesses.
  - Course material as yardstick (2026-11-11): the marker's `course_knowledge` (none / all / selected files; shown for the marker only, in the card "Wat leest de nakijkhulp?" and its editor) decides whether the pre-assessment searches the course material (`buildCourseQueries`: rubric criteria or short rubric lines plus a few windows of the product; `mergeCourseMatches`; numbered like the chat). The model then tests each criterion against that material and cites it per criterion (`course_evidence`, only source numbers that were given). When the setting is on but nothing matches, it gets the same rule: no own knowledge as yardstick, content doubts go under `attention` (`courseNoMatch` is shown to the teacher). Off = unchanged. A new marker knows the rubric, the assignment and other project documents and all course material by default; existing markers stayed at none. Step 4 starts with "Hoe wil je feedback en beoordeling?" (marking only / both / feedback only), derived from what exists; it only shows and hides steps and warns about leftovers, it deletes nothing.
  - Grounded: quotes that do not literally occur in the product are dropped (`verifyQuotes`); instructions inside the product are ignored and reported under `attention`.
  - Only course teachers and LEAP admins see it (submissions are deliberately shared work). Students only see a notice that it happens. The evaluator for the marking comments is excluded from student feedback rounds, the room's evaluator list and "Feedback op jullie aanpak", so its comments stay with the teacher.
  - Downloads: per submission a Word file (`…/preassessment.docx`), per project one zip (`…/submissions-export.zip`: per group the product + pre-assessment, plus an overview CSV). Check with `node --env-file=.env scripts/check-preassessment.mjs [--long]` (real model, test account, cleans up).

### Learning levels and journals in projects
- Learning levels play no role in projects. The project room has no level selector or readiness button, and the persona chat ignores `learningLevel`.
- Since 2026-10-27 the project NEVER writes to the journals of all group members. The texts stay with the group (`group_checkpoints.journal_items`, `project_document_reviews.journal_entry`, `project_group_assessments`; migration `20261027140000_project_journal_on_request` + rollback) and each member saves what they want to their OWN journal with "Bewaar in mijn leerdagboek" (`POST /api/projects/groups/:groupId/journal-save`, `server/projects/projectJournalSave.js`; one `source_ref` per item and user, older automatic refs count as saved; at the final reflection a member can add an own reflection). A finished group stays reachable read-only (`finishedGroup` in the student overview). `server/__tests__/projectJournalSave.test.js` and `server/__tests__/projectJournal.test.js` guard this.
- Project texts (interim checkpoint, final reflection, overview across conversations, summary per persona conversation, evaluator feedback rounds, "Feedback op jullie aanpak") use the journal blocks; structured input is converted without an extra LLM call (`server/projects/projectJournal.js`). See `docs/decisions/leren-met-ai.md`.
- Evaluator reports ("Feedback op jullie aanpak") are stored in `project_group_assessments` and shown in the project room; a member can save them to their own journal only when the journal switch is on (see `docs/decisions/cursusmateriaal-en-rag.md`).

### Project groups (2026-10-27)
- Each project has `group_mode` (`server/projects/projectGroups.js`, migration `20261027160000_project_group_modes` + rollback): `canvas` (default for canvas/canvas_open courses), `teacher` (default for open courses) or `self` (students form groups with an invite code; the old behaviour). `allow_self_signup` is kept in sync (= self) for old code.
- With `canvas` or `teacher` a student can never create, join, leave or restart a group (`studentMayFormGroups` guards `POST /api/projects/groups`, `/groups/join`, `/student-restart`; the UI hides those actions). The course teacher/admin manages groups via `/api/projects/:projectId/group-admin` (`ProjectGroupsAdmin`: create, rename, delete empty, assign/move, auto-divide unassigned students).
- The browser never creates or changes `project_groups` or `project_group_members` itself; the server does it with all checks (migration `20261030100000_security_hardening`). A teacher is never put into a student group via the server, and group management responses never contain invite codes; invite codes come from a cryptographic random source.
- Assigning (2026-11-11, `ProjectGroupsAdmin` + `ProjectStudentsDialog`): the student list is not on screen by default. The card shows the count ("8 students, 3 not yet assigned") and a button "Studenten indelen" that opens a pop-up: (1) random split of the unassigned students over new groups of at most N (default: the project's max group size; `splitIntoGroups` shuffles and uses ceil(n/N) groups, so no group exceeds N); (2) search, select (filter "only without a group") and put the selection in an existing group, a new group, or out of its group. With no students in the course the pop-up points to Members → invite students. Each group card keeps its own "add student" list; moving or removing also happens on the member row.
- Teacher test group (2026-11-11): a teacher of the course (or a LEAP admin) can always start their own group of one on the Projects page, also in an open project where the teacher or Canvas assigns students (`student-overview` returns `tryout`; the page then shows "Probeer uit in je eigen testgroep" and "start over"). The server already allowed staff to create a group; the group is named "Testgroep van …". Nobody else gets in: students cannot join a group with a course teacher via a code (`groups/join`), and group management refuses it (`teacherGroup`). Live check: `scripts/check-project-groups.mjs`.
- `PUT /api/projects/:projectId/group-admin/members` takes `{ userIds, groupId | null }` and is all-or-nothing on the checks: every id must be a member of the project's course, a non-student is never put in a group, and a group that contains a teacher of the course (the teacher's own test group, `teacherGroup` in the overview) never receives students. Live check: `scripts/check-project-groups.mjs`.
- Canvas group assignments (project link with `&project=<p>`): see `docs/decisions/toegang-en-canvas.md`. `server/__tests__/projectGroups.test.js`, `server/__tests__/lti.test.js`.
